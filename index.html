06 / About

Groove first.

The Silver Saints are a groovy six-piece band hailing from Oxford, Mississippi, most recognizable for their iconic female front.

Their sound blends retro-rock and jazz-funk with soulful grooves, resulting in shows that can’t help but make you move.
